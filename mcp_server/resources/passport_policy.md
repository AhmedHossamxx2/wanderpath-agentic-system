# Wanderpath Travel B. - International Passport & Entry Regulations

1. **Minimum Validity Requirement**:
   - All international flight bookings require a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the departure date.
   - For European Union (Schengen Area) destinations, passports must have been issued within the last 10 years at the time of entry.

2. **Expired Passports**:
   - Bookings associated with expired passports MUST be flagged immediately.
   - No flight ticket issuance or international hotel check-in can proceed with an expired passport record.

3. **Visa Exemption & Restrictions**:
   - US and UK passport holders traveling to Schengen nations require valid travel authorization (ETIAS).
   - Non-refundable flight fares cannot be waived due to passport expiration errors unless senior manager authorization is granted.
