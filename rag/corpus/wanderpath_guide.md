# Wanderpath Travel B. - Internal Boutique Hotel Binder & Destination Guide

## Section 1: Alpine Resort & Spa (Switzerland)
- **City**: Zermatt
- **Country**: Switzerland
- **Category**: Hotel Policy
- **Cancellation Window**: Standard cancellation window is 14 days prior to check-in. Cancellations within 14 days incur a 50% penalty.
- **Pet Policy**: Service animals are welcome with advance notification. Standard pets require a $50/night cleaning fee.
- **Fasting & Amenities**: Pre-spa treatments require a 2-hour fasting window.

## Section 2: Tokyo Grand Palace (Japan)
- **City**: Tokyo
- **Country**: Japan
- **Category**: Hotel Policy
- **Service Animals**: Tokyo Grand Palace fully accommodates service dogs across all suite tiers without additional deposits.
- **Advisory Notice**: Localized public transit strikes affecting the Yamanote line are scheduled for late 2026. Guests should utilize private shuttle transfers arranged via concierge.

## Section 3: Bali Sun & Sand Resort (Indonesia)
- **City**: Bali
- **Country**: Indonesia
- **Category**: Travel Advisory
- **Entry & Visa**: Visa on Arrival (VoA) required for US/UK citizens. Passports must have at least 6 months validity.
- **Policy**: All holiday package bookings during peak season (December) are strictly non-refundable.
